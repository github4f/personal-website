Farshad Firouzi

From Silicon to Space

Farshad Firouzi is a faculty member in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Johns Hopkins University, a member of the Data Science and AI Institute, and a technology entrepreneur. His work spans the full AI computing stack—from emerging devices, microelectronics, and AI accelerators to foundation models, agentic AI, and intelligent computing infrastructure. He advances cross-layer innovations to develop efficient, scalable, secure, and trustworthy AI systems for real-world applications, including physical and embodied AI, autonomous systems, and space computing and communications.
